## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Post the REACTION, not the product** — people share emotions, not features
2. **Ride every holiday** — create content 2 weeks before each occasion
3. **UGC is gold** — ask every user to film their recipient's reaction
4. **The viral loop IS your marketing** — every video sent = free advertising
5. **$10/day on ads** with good creative will outperform $100/day with bad creative
6. **Comment on trending posts** about gifting, birthdays, relationships — add value, drop your link naturally
7. **Respond to EVERY comment and DM** in the first month — builds community fast
