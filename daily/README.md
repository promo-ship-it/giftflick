# 30 Days of Empowerment — Daily Affirmation Audio

Morning & Evening affirmations for the woman I am becoming.

Each daily MP3 contains **two chapters** — **Morning** and **Night** — so you can skip
straight to whichever you need. Chapter navigation works in most podcast/audiobook players
(Apple Podcasts, Overcast, VLC, AntennaPod, Podcast Addict, etc.).

- **Voice:** Australian (en-AU, natural neural voice "Natasha")
- **Format:** MP3 with embedded chapter markers

control (often the same buttons as skip track, or a chapter list menu).
