## ❌ WHAT WE DON'T SAY

| Avoid | Why | Say Instead |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| "Cheap" | Devalues the product | "Affordable" or "less than a coffee" |
| "AI-generated" alone | Sounds cold | "AI-powered" or "AI magic" |
| "Bot" or "automated" | Sounds impersonal | "Personalized" or "created for you" |
| "Unlimited" (for Pro) | It's capped at 50 | "Up to 50 videos/month" |
| "Replace gifts" | Sounds anti-gift | "Level up your gifting" |
